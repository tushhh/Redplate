Eleven screens, one instrument, softened at the edges.

Read the repo before writing this: CLAUDE.md, COACHING.md, SCREENS.md, the theme files, SetLoggingScreen.kt and PlateStack.kt. The design language is already unusually well specified, so this plan changes as little of it as possible and spends its budget on the ten screens that don't exist yet.

Nothing below is built yet. Read it, strike what you disagree with, then I start on screens.

01

What I'm assuming

CONSTRAINTS HOLD

Offline, local-only, no accounts, no network, portrait, one device, kg internally. Every screen I design must work with zero connectivity and zero server.

SHIPPABLE-ISH

02

Softening the instrument

You asked for warmer and rounder; the spec asks for instrument-grade. My read: the coldness isn't the black, it's that everything is a hairline box on a void. So I keep the discipline and change four things.

01
Ground lifts off true black. #000 → #0C0E11. Still AMOLED-cheap and glare-free, but surfaces can now sit above something.
02
Elevation replaces hairlines. Two surface steps instead of borders everywhere. Borders become a highlight for the one live thing.
03
Radius up. 10dp controls → 14dp; cards 18dp; sheets 26dp. Rounder reads as approachable without reading as toy.
04
Warmth lives in copy, not hue. "Nice — that's a PR" on the rest screen; "SET 3/4 · 8 REPS · 2 RIR" during the set. Clinical when working, human when waiting.

03

The six calls you left to me

Each of these is a decision, not a preference — overrule any of them and I'll rework the affected screens.

INTAKE
Two-tier, 60 seconds to a plan
Four questions get you a plan: goal · days per week · session length · how long you've been lifting. One question per screen, huge targets, no keyboard. Equipment comes next as its own step because it gates exercise selection, then bodyweight, then the readiness screen as a single card you can answer with one tap. Optional depth (known 1RMs, injuries, muscle priorities, disliked exercises) is never in the first run — it's offered from Profile and prompted contextually ("you've logged bench 3× — want to set a target 1RM?"). Rationale: COACHING.md's six required fields would be a six-screen wall before you've seen anything work.
DIFFICULTY INPUT
Plain-language chips that are RIR
Five 64dp chips replace the −/+ RIR stepper: EASY(4+) · 3 LEFT · 2 LEFT · 1 LEFT · ALL OUT(0), plus a sixth FAILED state that logs the rep count honestly and tells the engine to back off. Each chip shows its RIR number in small type so you learn the vocabulary, and Settings has "show RIR numbers only" for when you no longer need the words. A stepper is two taps and an abstraction; a chip is one tap and a sentence.
WEIGHT SUGGESTION
Pre-filled, dominant, and it shows its work
The prescribed load is the plate stack — already the biggest thing on screen — and it arrives filled in. Under it, one line of reasoning in plain language: "+2.5 kg — you hit 10 reps at 2 left on every set last time." Tapping the line expands the full rule (which progression rule, what history it used, what happens next). Steppers stay for overriding, and an override is never scolded — it becomes input. This is the differentiator in COACHING.md made visible; a suggestion you can't interrogate is the black box you're paying other apps for.
NAVIGATION
Four tabs that vanish mid-session
TODAY · PLAN · HISTORY · YOU as a 64dp bottom bar. The moment a session starts the bar is replaced by the session's own controls — the set screen is full-bleed and single-purpose, exactly as specced, and leaving a live workout is a deliberate act, not a mis-tap. Exercise browser and guidance are sheets, not tabs, because you always reach them from something. No hamburger, no FAB, no drawer.
REST DAY / HOME
One answer to "what do I do today?"
Today always leads with a single card that answers the question — on a training day it's the session with its slot list and an 88dp START bar; on a rest day it's "Rest. Next: Lower B, tomorrow" with the same card greyed and a secondary "train anyway" path. Under it: a 7-day strip showing where you are in the week, then this week's volume as plate-coloured bars. Recovery status is context, never the headline — nobody opens a gym app to read a dashboard.
BODY MAP
Yes — 9 regions now, 16 later
It's the signature interaction and it doubles as the volume dashboard, so it's worth the work. But SCREENS.md's 16-region analysis ends in padded hit-rects, priority-order hit-testing, fuzzy trigger bands and one muscle that can't be hit at all — that's a lot of machinery to validate on a first pass. I'll ship 9 comfortable regions (chest, back, shoulders, arms, core, glutes, quads, hams, calves), each shaded MEV/MAV/MRV, each opening a sheet that lists the fine-grained muscles inside it. The 16-region map then becomes a refinement of a working screen rather than a prerequisite. Your two SVGs in design/ are the source geometry either way.
04

Screens I'll design

Eleven screens plus states. Grouped into three rounds so you're reviewing something within one pass, not eleven screens at once.

ROUND 1The spine — what a first-time user hits, and what you use every session. 3+ variations each.
Intake flow
Goal · frequency · session length · training age. Plus the equipment step: searchable, categorised, with the "which dumbbells do you actually have" loading detail.
Today
Training day, rest day, mid-session-resume and first-run-empty states. The card that answers "what do I do today?".
Set logging, revamped
Softened surfaces, difficulty chips, reasoning line, one-tap swap. Plus the rest state at 112sp and the PR moment.
ROUND 2Finding and understanding exercises.
Exercise browser
Three tiers per SCREENS.md: Yours · body map · search. Bottom-anchored search, equipment filter on by default, unavailable exercises dimmed not hidden.
Body map + volume
Front/back, 9 regions shaded by weekly volume. Doubles as the per-muscle dashboard, so it's one screen not two.
Guidance sheet
Bottom sheet: muscles, steps, start/end stills, external video hand-off, ranked substitutes. Degrades to text-only when media is missing.
ROUND 3The long game — plan, edit, look back.
Weekly plan
The mesocycle week: which split, what's trained when, where the deload lands. Rescheduling is drag and a visible move control.
Program builder
Edit what the engine generated: swap exercises, change set counts, override a progression rule, see the volume consequence live as you edit.
History · PRs · e1RM
Session log in Plex Mono, PR list, e1RM curve per lift, plateau flags. Charts drawn in plate colours, not chart-library defaults.
05

What I deliberately won't do

No BMI, calories, goal weight or body-fat. COACHING.md §1 rules it out and I agree — progress is load, reps, consistency. "Weight loss" as a goal becomes a volume/rest/conditioning prescription, not a diet screen.
No streaks, badges, confetti or rings. One earned celebration: the PR moment. Gamifying attendance makes a missed week feel like failure, which is how people quit.
No hand-drawn exercise art. Where exercises.json media isn't resolved I'll use honest placeholders and a muscle glyph — never a broken image, never a fake illustration.
No new fonts, no gradients, no second accent. One warm accent per screen stays the rule. If two things look live, neither is.
